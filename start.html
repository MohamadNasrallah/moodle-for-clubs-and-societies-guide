Get started

Five steps from registering to taking part. Then see how an event can work on Nexus.

  1. Register for a club

    Use Academic Services > Registration.

  2. Open Nexus and find the club

    Clubs appear automatically under My Courses after you register.

  3. Start with the General section

    Get to know the club, its cabinet, and its latest news.

  4. Check Upcoming Events

    See what is coming next.

  5. Join in

    Sign up, vote, discuss, or share feedback.
