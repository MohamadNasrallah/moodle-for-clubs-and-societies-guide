IEEE AUB is our main example of how a club page can be structured.

Example page structure and ideas

IEEE AUB

These are ideas for how a club page could be set up. They are not a list of what is currently live.

Tap a section to see the idea.

Solve It Tuesday Idea

A weekly algorithm problem, in any language or pseudocode, with a discussion space and recognition for consistent participation.

Tech Weekly Idea

A Monday forum post with three tech stories summarized in the club’s own words, using IEEE Spectrum as the source.

Ideas for other clubs

These are ideas only. Each club decides what fits.

Debate

Vote on the next motion, then continue the debate in a forum.

Photography or arts

A shared gallery of member work.

Outdoors or sports

Trip sign-ups and a preparation checklist.

Cultural clubs

A shared glossary and a searchable event archive.

Technical clubs

Workshops, practice questions, and project teams.

These ideas are inspired by strong IEEE student branches elsewhere, such as PoliTO recording talks for members who missed them, and Brescia building activity around member projects.
