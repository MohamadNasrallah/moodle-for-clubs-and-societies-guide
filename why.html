Why Nexus matters

Club pages on Nexus are meant to make clubs easier to find and easier to join in with.

  • 1Attract more students to clubs and societies
  • 2Increase engagement
  • 3Shift clubs from something you only follow to something you take part in
  • 4Make clubs interactive, with signing up, voting, learning, discussing, and building together
  • 5Keep each club’s events, resources, and history in one place so they carry forward from one cabinet to the next

Follow or take part?

Follow

Something you watch from the outside.

  • See what a club posts
  • Hear about an event
  • Stay a spectator
→

Take part

Something you are inside of.

  • Sign up and vote
  • Learn and discuss
  • Build together
