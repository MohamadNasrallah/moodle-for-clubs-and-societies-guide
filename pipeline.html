The pipeline

Register for a club, and it shows up on your Moodle page. Then take part. Here is the path, step by step.

  1. Register for a club

    Use Academic Services > Registration. This is the only step you do to get the club onto Moodle.

  2. Find it on your Moodle page

    After you register, the club appears automatically under My Courses.

  3. Start with the General section

    Get to know the club, its cabinet, and its latest news.

  4. Check Upcoming Events

    See what is coming next.

  5. Join in

    Sign up, vote, discuss, or share feedback.
